# Novelty Boundary — Compile, Don't Teach

Status: **NARROW / producer remediation complete; pending independent re-review**  
Work package: `WP-003 / ENG-155`  
As of: **2026-08-07**

## Decision

The search does not support `first`, `unique`, `unprecedented`, a general neuro-symbolic-method claim, a general compiler-runtime claim, or a general offline-compilation claim.

The independent review found omitted 2026 work and invalidated the previous saturation argument. The remediation adds SIGIL, FACTS, Skill Discovery, SkillRT, SkCC, Auto, Anything2Skill, skill-program work, Readout Shortcut and PROTEA, freezes a 51-source seven-dimension ledger, and reruns two candidate-level replacement saturation rounds. No fresh source reaches five strict `yes` values out of seven defining dimensions, but this remains producer-side search coverage rather than novelty acceptance.

No single reviewed primary source currently matches the complete surviving causal design. Almost every broad architectural component is occupied, so the contribution must be phrased as a positive matched comparison and independently reviewed before GATE-001 can move.

## Occupied territory

### Solver-aided and program-aided reasoning

PAL, Program of Thoughts, Faithful Chain-of-Thought, SatLM, Logic-LM, LINC, Binder/NLEP-style work and related systems already establish LM-generated programs or symbolic specifications, external execution, and gains from offloading computation or deduction. Correctness is commonly conditional on the parsed/generated specification.

The flagship may not present external deterministic reasoning, theorem proving, Prolog execution, or translation-solve-render decomposition as its invention.

### Typed compiler/runtime ownership and executable harnesses

**SIGIL (RW-042)** now occupies especially close territory. It compiles prose skills into a typed AG-IR, marks steps as model-owned or code-owned, preserves source provenance, applies compile gates, lowers deterministically into executable harnesses, and directly compares prose versus compiled execution under matched authoring behavior.

**SkillSmith (RW-038)** compiles skill packages offline into executable boundary contracts and demonstrates reuse of stronger-model compiled artifacts across smaller or more efficient runtime models and multiple harnesses.

**SkCC (RW-048), SkillRT (RW-045), Auto (RW-047), skill-program work (RW-046), and Anything2Skill (RW-049)** further occupy typed skill IRs, portable compiled artifacts, JIT/runtime solidification, executable intervention programs, verified reusable behavior, and knowledge-to-skill compilation.

The flagship therefore may not claim:

- the first compiler-runtime architecture for language agents;
- the first typed IR that separates model cognition from code mechanism;
- the first prose-to-executable agent harness compiler;
- the first stronger-model artifact reused by a weaker/cheaper runtime model;
- the first portable compiled agent skill or executable skill runtime;
- that moving repeated interpretation from model context into code is itself new;
- that `Compile, Don't Teach` names a new general paradigm.

### Formal semantics compiled into executable tools

Ontology-to-tools (RW-039) compiles formal ontological specifications into executable LLM-callable tools that enforce semantic constraints during knowledge-graph construction.

The flagship may not claim that formal knowledge or ontologies are newly made executable for LLM agents. Its remaining question is narrower: how an already computed authoritative epistemic result should be placed at the model/runtime boundary and rendered by a frozen small model.

### Offline deterministic and execution-verified artifacts

FACTS (RW-043) generates reusable SQL/Jinja2 templates offline and then executes queries/renders summaries deterministically. Skill Discovery (RW-044) uses offline simulation and execution feedback to curate reusable verified scripts for runtime automation.

The flagship may not claim novelty for generating executable artifacts offline, validating them before reuse, or reducing repeated runtime generation/interpretation.

### Tool use and modular systems

MRKL, ReAct, Toolformer, ART, Gorilla, ToolLLM, CRITIC and related work occupy routing, tool calls, observations, and modular neural/discrete systems.

Keeping a model fixed while calling an external tool is not by itself a contribution.

### Structured generation

PICARD, grammar-constrained decoding, LMQL, guided generation, SynCode, XGrammar, JSONSchemaBench and grammar-constrained logical parsing occupy syntactic/schema guarantees.

Typed JSON alone is not the contribution. Any result attributed to a computed conclusion must survive a structured-output condition that carries the same shape but withholds the authoritative conclusion.

### Prompt/program optimization and teaching

APE, OPRO, ProTeGi, DSPy, Promptbreeder, EvoPrompt, Self-Refine, CRITIC and related systems occupy automatic prompt generation, optimization and iterative refinement.

The flagship may measure bounded effects and failure modes of contextual teaching, but it may not imply that prompt/program optimization is new or universally ineffective.

### Training and verifier-guided reasoning

Weak-to-Strong Generalization, process supervision, Prolog-tool training and related systems occupy weight-changing teacher/student supervision and learned verification. Recent ACL 2026 work trains a 3B model to use Prolog.

The flagship's distinction is immutable weights and interface placement, not superiority to trained systems.

### Readout/copying shortcuts in small models

The Readout Shortcut (RW-050) shows that fixed 1–3B instruction-tuned models can copy answer-like trailing values rather than causally use intermediate reasoning.

This occupies an important alternative explanation. A conclusion-bearing compiled frame cannot count as evidence for the flagship mechanism if the renderer can succeed by copying a ready answer. The structured-no-conclusion and explicit answer-copying controls are therefore part of the contribution boundary, not optional robustness checks.

### Layer-wise workflow diagnosis

PROTEA (RW-051) scores intermediate workflow artifacts and localizes likely faulty nodes before targeted refinement.

The flagship may not claim generic layer-wise workflow diagnosis as new. Its narrower evaluator contribution is an independently implemented oracle/scorer aligned to its specific source/query/status/policy/frame/rendering contract.

## Positive remaining contribution

Subject to independent WP-003 review and later W0 gates, the candidate contribution is:

> A preregistered matched causal evaluation of **authoritative semantic-result placement** for fixed-weight small language models under a four-state strict epistemic contract, comparing contextual/raw, teacher-edited, structured-no-conclusion, trusted-executed, conclusion-bearing, and answer-copying conditions, with an independently implemented layer-specific oracle/scorer.

It has six inseparable parts:

1. **Matched causal comparison:** the executed/result-frame mode is compared with the DEV-selected strongest matched non-compiled representation, not Raw Prolog alone.
2. **Fixed-weight boundary:** student weights and frozen student artifacts are identical across conditions.
3. **Strict epistemic contract:** `supported`, `refuted`, `unknown`, and `conflicting` remain distinct and carry policy/provenance/warning/evidence obligations.
4. **Authoritative-result placement:** the experiment changes where the computed semantic conclusion enters the pipeline rather than merely adding tools or a compiler.
5. **Shortcut controls:** structured/no-conclusion and answer-copying conditions isolate execution from format, extra information, and ready-answer copying.
6. **Independent layer evaluation:** source, query, runtime status, policy, frame transport, and rendering are scored separately without importing the production compiler as oracle.

Removing any one of these makes the claim substantially more likely to collapse into occupied territory.

## Viability conditions

The central claim survives only if:

- baseline selection is frozen on DEV;
- the scenario-level clustered effect is practically meaningful;
- the direction replicates independently;
- structured/no-conclusion and answer-copying controls do not explain the result;
- no single model/domain/status/source family creates the pooled effect;
- independent oracle/scorer and mutation audits pass;
- renderer obligations remain non-trivial after authoritative execution;
- the manuscript differentiates the result directly from SIGIL, SkillSmith, ontology-to-tools, FACTS, Skill Discovery, LINC, Logic-LM, Faithful CoT, SatLM, Readout Shortcut and the newer skill compilers.

Null or negative evidence forces either a narrower failure-analysis contribution or abandonment of the flagship causal claim.

## PIVOT / STOP

**PIVOT before scaling** if a refresh finds a paper that combines fixed-weight small students, contextual/raw/teacher-edited interfaces, trusted executed semantic results, typed four-state epistemic states, matched structure/copying controls, and independent layer evaluation.

**STOP the flagship route** if the only favorable comparison is solver versus no solver; the result-frame mode loses to the strongest matched control; copying/ready-answer conditions explain the effect; evaluator independence fails; replication reverses; or the paper requires universal/privacy/learning/priority wording to appear novel.

## Saturation boundary

The former Q9/Q10 saturation conclusion is withdrawn as legacy evidence because its candidate-level screening trail was not frozen reproducibly. The remediation instead uses two fresh replacement rounds, Q11 and Q12, after the 51-source dimension ledger and candidate screening ledger were frozen. Neither round found a source with at least five strict `yes` values across the seven defining dimensions or an exact match to the surviving positive comparison.

This supports only **provisional producer-side search saturation**. It is evidence of search coverage, never proof of absence. Independent review remains mandatory, and the search must be refreshed before final claim freeze and immediately before submission.
