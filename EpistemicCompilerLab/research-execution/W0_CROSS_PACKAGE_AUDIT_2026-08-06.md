## 2. Cross-package Blockers

### C1 — Primary causal identity drift

Current identifiers are inconsistent:

- WP-004: `C-PRIMARY`, M6 versus M14, `E-PRIMARY-COMPILED-BUNDLE`, baseline rule `DEV-GLOBAL-STRONGEST-MATCHED-V2`;
- WP-006: M6 minus M14 but baseline rule `V1`;
- WP-002: generic strongest-matched wording without exact accepted identifiers.

No package may pass until exact contrast, treatment, comparator, estimand, endpoint, unit, baseline version and retention rule agree byte-for-byte or through one canonical registry.

### C2 — Headline mechanism boundary

WP-004 correctly states that the primary contrast is a multi-component deployed-interface bundle. WP-002 and the original ENG-156 acceptance can still be read as isolating compilation/runtime as one factor.

Required governance decision:

- headline = total verified-interface bundle effect;
- component/mechanism claims = secondary falsification contrasts only;
- no abstract sentence attributes the primary effect uniquely to solver execution, typing, conclusion or rendering.

### C3 — Mode contract drift

The normative roadmap defines M10 as minimal `status/action`; current WP-004 defines status-only. Until reviewed:

- no validator may treat M10 semantics as frozen;
- WP-002 copying claims and WP-006 mode costs remain provisional;
- feasibility must use a mode-count range, not one final call total.

### C4 — Oracle/gold/scorer circularity

WP-005 must separate:

- B-oracle computation packet with no expected final outcomes;
- independently governed gold registry;
- B-scorer visibility after freeze.

Feasibility must not count these as one interchangeable agent/session or omit independent human/adjudication effort.

### C5 — Statistical decision mismatch

WP-006 reports power for superiority over zero at a true effect of 0.08 but also requires an observed effect at least 0.08. The 600-scenario recommendation is provisional until one coherent decision rule is chosen and re-simulated with source-family clustering, realistic failures, joint replication and Monte Carlo uncertainty.

WP-007 must therefore evaluate sample-size and mode-count ranges, not assert 600 as accepted.

### C6 — Related-work saturation invalidated

WP-003 omitted a materially nearest July 2026 typed-harness compiler paper and lacks a reproducible screening/dimension ledger. No novelty/priority wording is authorized. Feasibility may estimate work for a narrowed causal study only; it must not assume the current title or architecture is itself novel.

### C7 — Semantic validators missing

WP-002…WP-006 currently expose generic artifact wrappers rather than dedicated semantic validators. GATE-001 must not accept self-reported producer checks that cannot be independently rerun from committed code.

Each package must provide:

- one non-mutating reviewer command;
- machine-readable output;
- cross-file and cross-package invariants;
- fail-closed behavior on known review mutations;
- exact committed handoff with current hashes.
